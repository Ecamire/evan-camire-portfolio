We Handle Everything For You
Planning a trip can feel like one more job on a long list. That is where we come in. We handle the motorcoach, the hotel rooms, and all your group dining. We can even help with fundraising and collect the payments for you. All your group leader has to do is gather the group and enjoy the ride. Let us take the hard part off your plate, so you can look forward to the trip.
Fellowship Along The Way
Good trips are made better by good company. Our senior groups ride together, eat together, and laugh together the whole way there. Many of our travelers say the fellowship is their favorite part of the trip. Whether it is a long table of good Southern food or a quiet ride through the mountains, your group will make memories side by side. That is what we love most about this work.
